# ShadowBox judge demo — truthful four-minute runbook

**Demo promise:** fit a controller-free straight-punch drill to a boxer, teach one observable movement with a procedural ghost glove, then reuse the same live calibration in a reaction board without leaving the mixed space.

**Software evidence:** the quiet 05:38 snapshot passed 111/111 tests and clean generic Simulator/unsigned arm64 visionOS builds. **Validation boundary:** this runbook does not claim that a signed build, live headset path, spatial audio latency, accessibility, or tracking accuracy has passed. Mark each device item on demo day. If a live gate fails, use the labelled fallback immediately; never imply Simulator or pre-recorded behavior is a live sensor result.

## Status labels for the team

- **SOFTWARE-VERIFIED:** behavior belongs to the exact quiet 05:38 snapshot and its recorded 111/111 test plus clean generic-build evidence; physical-headset acceptance is still required.
- **LIVE-VERIFIED:** the exact demo build/path passed on the physical device, in the venue setup, on demo day.
- **RECORDED-DEVICE:** a clearly dated physical-device recording; not live.
- **SIMULATOR:** UI/state demonstration only; it cannot prove live hand/device tracking, fit accuracy, spatial audio, comfort, or safety.
- **FUTURE:** roadmap concept, not part of the demo.

Do not change a label to **LIVE-VERIFIED** from source review, unit tests, Simulator, or an earlier build.

## Non-negotiable claim boundary

### Say

- “Vision Pro observes tracked hand joints and headset pose. ShadowBox derives hand path, guard, functional reach, and timing from those observations.”
- “The fit check uses two controlled extensions per hand and accepts a conservative reach only when the pair is sufficiently consistent.”
- “Execution pace is retained only as an internal diagnostic. It is not displayed, scored, coached, persisted, used by the current recommendation policy, or treated as force.”
- “The difficulty control changes cue timing and visual guidance. Geometry, score weights, and safety rules stay fixed.”
- “The next-level suggestion is transparent, rules-based, session-evidence-driven, and user-applied.”
- “The procedural ghost glove shows a reference path. It does not mean the app tracked an elbow, shoulder, torso, or hip.”
- “Aura will not start unless both hands are tracked. Defense cancels excessive-range evidence and requires neutral plus explicit resume/fresh countdown.”
- “The profile and explicit preferences are stored locally. Motion evidence, recommendations, and attempt results remain session-only.”

### Never say

- force, power, impact, effective mass, calories, damage, protection, injury prevention, or medical measurement;
- full-body, hip, waist, pelvis, knee, footwork, balance, ground-reaction, or plantar-force tracking from AVP;
- professional technique validation, coach replacement, sparring competence, combat readiness, or proven skill transfer;
- “AI persona.” The current advisor is deterministic rules; Apple Persona represents a real participant, not an NPC. A custom 3D coach is future work;
- native Vision Pro haptics;
- live device validation, market traction, efficacy, or final test counts that have not actually been recorded.

## Four-minute live path

The timings assume the exact path has been rehearsed and marked **LIVE-VERIFIED** that day. Otherwise use the same narrative over the correctly labelled fallback.

### 0:00–0:20 — the whole product in one sentence

**Show:** the Boxer/Boxing Trainer home with Anthropometry, Aura Punch, and Reactive Strike.

**Say:**

> “ShadowBox is a controller-free spatial fundamentals lab: fit the drill to this boxer, show one observable movement, practise it, and explain only what Vision Pro actually measured.”

Do not open every feature. Point once to Reactive Strike's Board, Defense, and non-contact Bag Preview as bounded modes, then continue to Fit.

### 0:20–0:50 — profile and measurement honesty

**Show:** an already prepared, plausible local boxer profile and its consistency panel. Do not spend stage time typing every dimension.

**Say:**

> “Manual height, span, arm lengths, shoulder width, stance, and dominant hand personalize the profile and catch inconsistent entry. They do not improve ARKit tracking. The live functional fit is what places today's targets.”

Point to the local-data explanation:

> “Only explicit profile scalars and preferences persist. Per-hand repetitions, world-space motion, recommendations, and results stay in this session.”

Confirm the controlled-submaximal safety acknowledgement before entering mixed space.

### 0:50–1:40 — controlled bilateral functional fit

**Show in mixed passthrough:** both hands at guard; tracking status visible; no surrounding obstacle.

**Action:**

1. Hold both fists still at a comfortable guard until accepted.
2. Extend and return one fist twice, smoothly and submaximally, while the other stays at guard.
3. Complete the other fist's two controlled extensions.
4. Show each hand's repeat count/spread status and the conservative bilateral value if accepted.

**Say:**

> “Each hand is checked independently. The fit accepts the shorter repetition only when the pair agrees within a provisional repeatability allowance, then uses the minimum accepted left/right reach for the local profile. This is a functional placement check—not anatomy, clinical measurement, or proof of device accuracy.”

If a hand is rejected, make **one** calm retry. If it rejects again or tracking is unstable, exit to the recorded fallback; do not rush, overextend, or manually force acceptance.

### 1:40–2:40 — ghost-glove Aura lesson

**Show:** Jab selected, procedural ghost glove and path visible, three controlled repetitions.

**Action:** watch the guide, follow with one jab, return to captured guard; repeat three times without accelerating for spectacle.

**Say during the first guide:**

> “The ghost glove is an original procedural reference. The overall score uses observed hand-path alignment, extension, and the other hand at guard; return is explained separately. Execution pace is internal and never rewarded.”

If the separately labelled **Trajectory shape · diagnostic** appears, say:

> “This is a fail-closed geometric comparison of complete, normalized paths. It is session-only and excluded from coaching and the overall score; it is not a professional-technique grade.”

After the set, point to one positive cue and one focus cue:

> “Feedback is deterministic and offline, so the reason is inspectable. We correct one high-confidence hand behavior at a time; we do not invent hip or elbow measurements.”

Do not claim the animation is a qualified-coach-approved professional technique unless that review has actually occurred.

### 2:40–3:35 — same-space transfer to Punch Board

**Show:** the in-space **Continue to Punch Board** action.

**Action:** transfer without closing the immersive space; keep both hands tracked and at guard; complete a few clear jab/cross cues using controlled extensions. If the 60-second round cannot finish within the slot, use **Stop & Exit** after the desired evidence rather than rushing.

**Say:**

> “This handoff is the product moment: the board reuses the same live guard, per-hand reach, forward frame, and chosen level because the mixed immersive session never closes.”

> “Each pad also labels jab or cross; a logical cue—not a sound or rendered frame—is scoring truth. The result separates hit, wrong hand, miss/timeout, response time, guard return, and tracking state. Execution pace is not shown or rewarded.”

Do not hit a physical object. If a wrong-hand or miss occurs naturally, use its explicit explanation as a trust feature; do not stage unsafe speed to guarantee a hit.

### 3:35–4:00 — value and boundary close

**Show:** the compact result/explanation state, sound mute, and Stop & Exit.

**Say:**

> “The competitive edge is not a bigger claim. It is a coherent Fit → Learn → React loop that refuses false precision: controller-free spatial practice, explainable local adaptation, and explicit unavailable states when the sensors cannot support a metric.”

> “Next is physical-headset acceptance and qualified content review—not pretending this measures force or full-body biomechanics. Optional iPhone, belt IMU, Watch, and instrumented-bag research add metrics only after their own validation gates.”

Stop and exit safely.

## Optional Defense — only after the four-minute path

Use only if the main path finished cleanly, the mode was separately rehearsed on the physical device, the demonstrator is comfortable, and the judge asks to see it.

- Keep feet planted; calibrate neutral head/device position.
- Run one no-score rehearsal, then at most two slow, clearly telegraphed slip/duck cues.
- Say: “This is a headset-motion proxy for stationary cue response. It does not score feet, hips, knees, balance, or defensive competence.”
- Say: “Visual and spatial-audio cues share the calibrated user-relative basis; tracking/system and safety-range pauses are reported separately.”
- The cue must dissolve before the headset. Abort on startle, uncertainty, boundary/person entry, or tracking loss.
- If the safety-range pause triggers, stop, return to neutral, and either end the optional demo or explicitly resume into its fresh countdown. Never try to score the return path.

Do not substitute Bag Preview for a striking demo. It is a static, non-contact visualization only.

## Fail-safe abort matrix

| Trigger | Immediate action | What the presenter says |
|---|---|---|
| Person, obstacle, cable, pet, or moving object enters the area | Stop movement; **Stop & Exit** | “We fail closed when the physical area changes.” |
| Pain, dizziness, nausea, discomfort, fear/startle, headset slip, or demonstrator request | Stop immediately; remove/settle headset safely; no retry | “Participant comfort overrides the demo.” |
| One/both hands or device pose unavailable/stale | Hold guard; allow automatic pause; do not count a miss | “Missing evidence pauses scoring; it never becomes a bad punch.” |
| Defense exceeds the controlled head-motion range | Stop; return to neutral; explicit resume/fresh countdown or exit | “The safety-range gate cancelled that evidence; the unsafe path cannot score.” |
| Tracking does not recover promptly | Stop & Exit; switch to labelled fallback | “Live tracking is not stable enough to support this attempt.” |
| Bilateral fit rejected twice | Do not stretch farther or loosen thresholds; fallback | “The repeatability gate refused this fit, so we will not place targets from weak evidence.” |
| Ghost glove/path absent or visibly misplaced | Stop the Aura set; fallback | “The reference is not trustworthy in this state.” |
| Audio absent, clips, or confuses the demonstrator | Mute; continue only with clear visual/text cues | “Audio is optional and never scoring truth.” |
| Board transfer does not start on first safe retry | Remain on Aura result or fallback; no rapid tapping | “We will not conceal a failed state transition.” |
| Boundary warning, recentering, interruption, or app error | Stop & Exit; fallback | “The spatial frame changed, so calibration is no longer evidence.” |

## Fast judge answers

**Where is the AI?**
“The MVP recommendation is intentionally rules-based and explainable. Core ML is a future gate only if a consented, participant-separated evaluation beats fixed and rule baselines. The ghost glove is authored guidance, not an AI Persona.”

**Does it measure punch power?**
“No. Hand motion is not physical contact. Force/effective mass needs synchronized instrumented contact and acceleration; we report hand-path and timing evidence only, while execution pace remains internal.”

**Does it track the whole body?**
“No. AVP gives hands and device pose. iPhone body pose and a belt IMU are optional research layers; feet and force still require dedicated evidence.”

**Can I hit my bag?**
“Not in this MVP. Bag Preview is non-contact. Real-bag use needs moving alignment, contact truth, clearance, and headset safety validation—or an iPhone workout without wearing Vision Pro.”

**Is the 3D coach already here?**
“Aura has an original procedural ghost glove. A licensed rigged coach/avatar and bounded defensive partner are roadmap items after animation, content, comfort, and device review.”

**What persists?**
“Explicit scalar profile and UI preferences are local. Motion evidence, recommendations, attempts, transforms, and results are session-only.”
